# Ali Welekhasia — Official Personal Website

Official personal website of **Gospel Minister & Worship Artist Ali Welekhasia** featuring Swahili worship music releases, interactive lyrics and chords, video sermons, spiritual devotionals, upcoming revival crusade countdowns, global ministry reach maps, online partnership giving, voice prayer requests, praise reports, and automated SEO syndication feeds.

## Core Features & Capabilities

- **Personal Music Ministry & Discography**: Interactive music players, categorized track listings, multi-tab lyrics (Swahili, Chords & Progression, English Translation), and chord chart PDF downloads.
- **Revival Crusades & Event Tracker**: Live countdown to open-air revival meetings, crusade location details, program schedules, and calendar integrations.
- **Devotionals & Spiritual Articles**: Inspirational teachings, scripture breakdowns, reading time estimations, and one-click social sharing to WhatsApp, Facebook, X, and direct copy.
- **Dynamic Sitemap (`sitemap.xml`) & RSS Feeds (`rss.xml`)**: Automated real-time XML generation for Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and RSS feed readers to accelerate indexing of new songs, sermons, and crusade notices.
- **Testimonies & Praise Wall**: Interactive auto-playing carousel with category filtering (*Music Ministry*, *Crusade Impact*, *Healing & Miracles*) and instant praise report submission modal.
- **Voice & Written Prayer Request Center**: Real-time microphone audio recording with interactive waveform visualizer, audio playback, draft persistence in `localStorage`, and submission to intercessors.
- **Interactive Global Reach Visualizer**: D3.js powered geographic reach map with country/town impact statistics.
- **Kingdom Partnership & Giving**: Multiple payment channels (M-Pesa Paybill / Till Number, Bank Transfer, PayPal, Credit Card) with instant copy assistance.
- **Theme & Performance**: Light/Dark theme switching, responsive edge-to-edge layouts, and accessible UI.
