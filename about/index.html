Ali Welekhasia

Gospel Artist & Worship Minister

The Calling & Ministry

Minister Ali Welekhasia is an anointed Kenyan gospel music artist, prolific songwriter, and evangelistic minister dedicated to leading souls to the knowledge and majesty of Jesus Christ. Born with a profound passion for God's presence, his musical journey is anchored in heartfelt praise, pure worship, and unapologetic proclamation of the Gospel.

Through anointed Swahili and Luhya worship songs such as Zawadi, Ni Wewe, Bado, Nasimama, Njooni Tumwimbie, Lamweli Umependwa, Nachenda Mushialo, Alibeba, Vumilia Moyo, and Umetenda Haya, his songs have touched thousands of believers across Kenya, East Africa, and the global diaspora.

Worship Philosophy & Biblical Heart

For Minister Ali, gospel worship is not merely musical artistry—it is a spiritual altar where heaven touches earth. Every composition is steeped in Scripture, declaring the holiness of God, the sacrifice of the Cross at Calvary, the power of His resurrection, and the blessed hope of His eternal kingdom.

Whether ministering before tens of thousands in open-air crusade grounds or serving local church congregations, his ministry focuses on calling people to repentance, physical and emotional healing through Christ, and steadfast faith in divine promises.

Open-Air Crusades & Mission Outreaches

Evangelism lies at the beating heart of Ali Welekhasia's calling. Through revival crusades held in Nairobi, Mombasa, the Lake Region, Eldoret, and surrounding towns, he partners with local pastoral bodies to stage mass evangelistic crusades equipped with powerful sound, fervent intercession, and personal ministry for those in need.
